🍽️ Our Story

We're on a mission to make
great food accessible to all

FreshBite started in a small Mumbai kitchen with a big dream — to connect food lovers with the city's best restaurants through technology, speed, and care.

The Beginning

From a small kitchen to a trusted local favourite

In 2018, two friends — Aryan and Priya — frustrated with cold, late deliveries, decided to build something better. They started FreshBite with just 5 restaurant partners and 3 delivery partners in Mumbai.

Today, FreshBite is the neighbourhood's go-to for quality food delivery, featuring a curated network of 8 hand-picked restaurants, 23 signature dishes, and a growing community of food lovers across the city.
