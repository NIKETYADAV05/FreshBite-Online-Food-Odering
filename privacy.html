This Privacy Policy describes how FreshBite Technologies Pvt. Ltd. ("FreshBite", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares information about you when you use our website, mobile application, and related services (collectively, the "Services"). By accessing or using our Services, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us and information collected automatically when you use our Services.

  • Account Information: Name, email address, phone number, and login credentials when you create an account.
  • Order Information: Items ordered, delivery addresses, order history, and payment method preferences.
  • Location Information: Delivery address and, if you enable it, real-time device location to recommend nearby restaurants.
  • Device & Usage Information: IP address, browser type, pages visited, referring URLs, and interaction data collected via cookies.

How We Use Your Information

  • Process and deliver your food orders.
  • Provide, maintain, and improve our Services and features.
  • Send you order updates, promotional offers, and personalized recommendations (with your consent where required).
  • Process payments, prevent fraud, and enforce our Terms of Service.
  • Respond to your comments, questions, and customer service requests.
  • Analyze usage trends and improve the overall user experience.

Cookies & Similar Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to remember your preferences, understand how you use our platform, and deliver relevant offers. You can control cookies through your browser settings. However, disabling certain cookies may limit your ability to use some features of our Services.

Cookie Policy

FreshBite uses cookies and similar technologies to enhance your browsing experience, remember your preferences (such as dark mode and saved addresses), and understand how you interact with our platform.

  • Essential Cookies: Required for core functionality such as keeping your cart and login session active.
  • Preference Cookies: Remember your settings like theme preference and saved addresses.
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand usage patterns to improve our Services.
  • Marketing Cookies: Allow us to show you relevant offers and promotions (with your consent).

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. Note that some features may not work correctly if you disable essential cookies.

How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data in the following limited circumstances:

  • Restaurant Partners: We share your order and delivery details with the restaurant preparing your food.
  • Delivery Partners: We share your address and contact details with our delivery team to complete your order.
  • Service Providers: We work with trusted vendors who assist with payment processing, analytics, and customer support.
  • Legal Compliance: We may disclose information when required by law or to protect our legal rights.

Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including encryption, secure data transmission (HTTPS), and access controls to protect your personal information. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Rights & Choices

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access & Update: Review and update your account information anytime in your Profile settings.
  • Marketing Choices: Opt out of promotional emails or notifications via your Settings.
  • Data Deletion: Request deletion of your account and associated data.
  • Cookie Controls: Manage cookies through your browser preferences.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@freshbite.in.

Children's Privacy

Our Services are not directed to individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to remove such information.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by posting the updated policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date above. Your continued use of our Services after any changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
