Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

Most orders are delivered within 25–45 minutes depending on your location and the restaurant's preparation time. You can see estimated time on the restaurant page before ordering.

We accept Cash on Delivery, all UPI apps (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM), Credit/Debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay), and the FreshBite Wallet.

Orders can be cancelled within 2 minutes of placing. Once the restaurant confirms, cancellation is not possible. For modifications, contact support immediately after placing the order.

On the cart page, enter your promo code in the "Apply Promo Code" section and click Apply. The discount will be applied to your order total instantly.

After placing an order, go to "My Orders" in your profile to see the real-time status of your delivery. You'll also receive updates via the notification system.
